## Tax-year/version findings

- The proposal targets 2025 only. A rule must not use this authority set to decide a
  2026 transaction.
- Logical tests may remain stable, but meal percentages/exceptions, mileage rates,
  depreciation/bonus rules, Section 179 limits and phaseouts, vehicle limits,
  per-diem amounts, home-office parameters, and other dollar values can change.
- Public Law 119-21 changed 2025 depreciation and Section 179 material after some
  earlier summaries were issued. Publication 946 (2025) and final filing-season
  instructions must be reviewed as a unit; no numerical asset rule is proposed for
  automatic v1.
- The 2025 Schedule C instructions describe a $2,500 per-item/invoice de minimis
  safe-harbor threshold without an applicable financial statement and $5,000 with
  one, but the treatment requires a taxpayer election and book-policy facts. It is
  not a universal price threshold.
- Publication 587's 2025 simplified home-office method uses $5 per allowable square
  foot, generally limited to 300 square feet, subject to qualification, shared-use,
  part-year, and income rules.
- Notice 2025-05 sets the optional 2025 business mileage rate at 70 cents per mile.
  Eligibility, method history, commuting, substantiation, and actual-expense choices
  keep vehicle treatment in Tier C.
- Each eventual active rule needs an immutable version and tax-year range. New
  authority creates a new rule version; it never silently reinterprets a historical
  treatment. Re-evaluation requires an explicit, auditable operation.
- Before activation, check source “Future Developments,” later legislation, notices,
  corrected publications, and form instructions again on the approval date.

## Substantiation findings

- All rules should preserve amount, date, source/item/service description, business
  purpose, business-use decision, exact allocation, and evidence history.
- Travel requires cost, dates, destination/place, business purpose, and trip context.
- Gifts require cost, date, description, recipient/business relationship, and annual
  direct/indirect recipient aggregation.
- Vehicle/transportation requires each use, mileage, business purpose, total annual
  miles, vehicle cost/improvements, and placed-in-service information as applicable.
- Meals require the establishment/date/amount and qualification facts; food at an
  entertainment event must be separately purchased or stated to escape the
  entertainment characterization.
- A receipt can support amount/date/character but does not alone establish purpose.
  Missing documentation is a separate risk signal and does not rewrite economics.

## Research exclusions and caution areas

No secondary blogs, accounting-firm marketing, merchant-category heuristics, or
repository legacy categories were treated as authority. State/local law, sales tax
collection, payroll, inventory/COGS, entity returns, Schedule E, filed forms, and
personal itemized deductions are outside this federal Schedule C v1 proposal.
