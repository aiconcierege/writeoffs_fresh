# Historical catch-up journey

Product authority: Rick's Catch-up Workflow V2 direction and September 24 scope clarification.
Implementation status and evidence: [V2 audit](audits/catch-up-v2/PLAN.md).

## One ledger, fixed coverage

Catch-up uses the original authorized/purchased historical interval. Covered ongoing
months never enter this journey merely because they age. For a September signup,
January–July is historical; unfinished August remains ongoing in October. The
rolling recent window affects priority, never pricing or purchased scope.

Current work has priority. These are orchestration scopes over the same canonical
transactions, decisions, evidence, allocations and reports—not separate books.

## Historical sequence

1. **Statements:** show the missing periods for statement/manual accounts. Offer
   upload, continue with available records, or later. Missing history never prevents
   work on supplied records or independent current activity.
2. **Receipts/documents:** invite useful purchase evidence. Known transfers, card
   payments, incoming money and statement-evidenced bank fees are not conventional
   receipt requests. A loan statement remains its distinct material workflow.
3. **More receipts:** upload does not mean collection is finished. Hold the requested
   document turn through processing and reassessment, then explicitly offer more,
   done or later. None/later are distinct immutable journey responses; neither
   fabricates a receipt, financial classification or customer tax fact.
4. **Personal exceptions:** at most 20 rows per turn, from business-only accounts.
   Confirming no exceptions records the review only. Unknown outgoing money can
   be identified as personal without first inventing an expense. A selected item
   uses the canonical personal correction, preserving its original source.
5. **Non-expense exceptions:** review proposed business expenses, excluding known
   non-P&L movements. A selected exception becomes unresolved economic activity
   with a material follow-up; WriteOffs does not invent a transfer or category.
6. **Remaining questions:** publish only current answerable facts not resolved by
   evidence or broad reviews. Uncertainty, deferral, refund relationships, loan
   splits and receipt matching retain their existing canonical safeguards.
7. **Done for now:** no question counter at zero; “You’re all set for now” and
   “Back to Home.” Missing source coverage remains separately visible.

Journey events do not rewrite financial history. Commands require ownership, MFA,
active capability, fixed scope, exact record/account-use versions and idempotent
request identity. Evidence acknowledgment validates owned immutable documents;
processing can make a former rendered stage obsolete without invalidating receipt.

## Reusable facts

Customer-confirmed recurring payouts are scoped to business, financial account,
counterparty, currency and effective date. Only materially consistent, unambiguous
payout/merchant-settlement evidence qualifies. Invoice-specific confirmations are
not generalized. Changed/conflicting evidence, customer corrections, pending sources
and revoked facts prevent reuse. The source answer and each dependent decision are
recorded. Customers can stop reuse in Deduction details without rewriting old books.

Existing merchant-scoped phone/internet percentages remain correctable facts.
A receipt cannot supply a business-use percentage. Evidence conflicts prevent blind
reuse. The customer gives plain facts; WriteOffs selects supported categories.

## Document interaction

One stable conversational processing state remains until authoritative review is
ready; moving on is deliberate. Upload acknowledgment means bytes are stored and a
job is durable, not that OCR/matching has finished. Slow analysis remains visible.

Mobile capture and multiple selection use normal browser capabilities. Explicitly
grouped receipt photos become one PDF with orientation-normalized viewing pages and
byte-identical original attachments. The grouping assertion is part of the source;
it never overrides conflicting amounts/dates or independent receipts. Unsupported
image decoding fails visibly without inventing extraction. See the audit for actual
verified formats and device/browser limits.

## Superseded historical behavior

This replaces historical specific-question-first routing, automatic completion of
receipt collection on upload, and “handled this visit” progress. Ongoing work does
not inherit repeated historical bulk reviews. “Questions left” counts only the
currently authoritative substantive set and can change with new evidence/answers.
